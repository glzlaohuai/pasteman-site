Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn’t here, get in touch below.

How do I open PasteMan?

Press ⇧⌘V in any app, or click the PasteMan icon in the menu bar. You can change the shortcut in Settings › General.

Why isn’t PasteMan recording what I copy?

Check that recording isn’t paused (menu bar icon › Pause Recording) and that the app you copied from isn’t in Settings › Privacy › Ignore Copies from These Apps. Passwords and other confidential content are never recorded. If macOS asks whether PasteMan may read the clipboard, choose Always Allow; the current setting is shown in Settings › Privacy.

Can PasteMan paste for me?

Yes. Turn on Settings › General › Paste into the Active App Automatically, then add PasteMan in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. Without it, the item you pick goes on the clipboard and you press ⌘V.

How do I sync between my Macs?

In Settings › Sync, turn on iCloud sync on each Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, or turn on local network sync and pair the Macs by confirming the code both show.

How do I see my Mac’s history on iPhone or iPad?

Install PasteMan on your iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud sync on there (it is on by default). On the Mac, turn on iCloud sync in Settings › Sync. On the same network you can also turn on local network sync on both, which works while PasteMan is open on the iPhone or iPad.

What are collections and smart collections?

A collection holds items you put in it (⌘S in the panel); they’re kept no matter how much history you keep. A smart collection is a saved search: filter the panel, then choose Save as Smart Collection from the ⌘F menu.

What does PasteMan Pro include?

Collections, smart collections, snippet macros and, on the Mac, editing before pasting (⌘E). It’s a one-time purchase for your Apple Account, shared by your Mac, iPhone and iPad; everything else in PasteMan is free. Already bought it on another device? Open Settings › PasteMan Pro and choose Restore Purchase.

How do snippet macros work?

Text kept in a collection can contain macros such as {date}, {clipboard} or {cursor}. When you paste it, PasteMan fills them in. Manage macros, add your own and pick what each inserts in Settings › Collections.

Which search shortcuts are there?

In the search field, type @ for an app, # for a kind of content (#image), . for a file extension (.pdf), / for a collection, or a date word such as “today” or “yesterday”, then press Tab or Return to turn it into a filter.

How do I delete my history?

Delete single items with ⌘⌫ in the panel, or clear everything in Settings › Privacy. With sync on, the history on your other devices is cleared too.
