Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

PasteMan is made by hiimobapps. In short: we don’t collect any of your data. PasteMan has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we run no servers that your clipboard could reach.

What stays on your Mac

PasteMan saves what you copy — text, images, links and references to files — in its own sandboxed storage on your Mac, so you can find and paste it again. You decide how much history is kept, and you can clear it at any time in Settings › Privacy.

Text in images and QR codes is recognized on your Mac. Content that apps mark as confidential or transient, such as passwords filled in by a password manager, is never recorded, and you can tell PasteMan to ignore any app.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync (it is off by default), your history is stored in your own private iCloud database under your Apple Account, so your other Macs can read it. We have no access to it.

The text and details of each item are stored in end-to-end encrypted iCloud fields. Large images and files use standard iCloud encryption, which is end-to-end when Advanced Data Protection is on. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. Turning sync off lets you delete the data PasteMan stored in iCloud.

Local network sync (optional)

If you turn on local network sync (it is off by default), your Macs exchange history directly over your network. Connections are end-to-end encrypted, devices that aren’t on the same iCloud account must be paired by confirming a code, and no server is involved.

Link previews

To show the title and icon of a link you copied, PasteMan connects directly to that website, and previews can load the page itself without keeping cookies or sign-ins. The website sees an ordinary request from your Mac. You can turn both off in Settings › General.

Permissions

Accessibility access is only needed if you turn on automatic pasting; it lets PasteMan press ⌘V for you after you pick an item. Local network access is only used for local network sync.

Children

PasteMan doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome through the support page.
