PasteMan
Features Privacy Support

Everything you copy, one keystroke away.

PasteMan quietly keeps your clipboard history on your Mac and brings any of it back the moment you need it.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 26 or later

Made for the way you copy and paste

⚡️

Instant, smooth panel

Press ⇧⌘V anywhere. Pick a card strip along any edge of the screen, a compact list at your cursor, or a centered search panel.

🔎

Find anything

Search the text you copied, the words in screenshots, link titles and QR codes. Type @app, #image, .pdf or “today” to filter as you go.

🗂

Collections and smart collections

Keep what matters in collections that never expire. Save a search as a smart collection that fills itself.

🧩

Snippet macros

Write {date}, {clipboard} or {cursor} in a snippet and PasteMan fills them in when you paste. Name your own macros in any language.

✍️

Paste it your way

Paste as plain text, change case, trim, encode or format JSON, edit before pasting, or paste several items together or one by one.

🔗

Links, images and files

See link titles and live page previews, open QR codes from screenshots, and keep files pasteable even after they move.

🔄

Sync your Macs

Sync through your own iCloud or directly over your local network with end-to-end encryption. No account to create.

🔒

Private by design

Your history stays on your Mac. Passwords from password managers are never recorded, and nothing is ever sent to us.

Thoughtful down to the details

  • Works entirely from the keyboard: ⏎ paste, ⌘1–9 quick paste, Space to preview, ⌘Z to undo a delete.
  • Short, quiet sounds and a light trackpad tap, all of which follow your system settings.
  • Drag cards straight into other apps or onto a collection.
  • Available in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français and Español.
